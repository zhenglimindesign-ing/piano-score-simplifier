# Source and redistribution basis

[中文](SOURCE.zh-CN.md) · [Example](README.md) · [Machine-readable record](SOURCE.json)

The notation authority is **G. Schirmer, New York, 1909, edited and fingered by Louis Oesterle, Library volume 909, plate 21360**, October on printed page 43. We use bars 1–16. The [NYPL / Internet Archive scan](https://archive.org/details/seasonsop37atwel00tcha) carries the original 1909 copyright and a 1937 renewal-assignment notice on its title page. [IMSLP's edition catalog](https://imslp.org/wiki/The_Seasons,_Op.37a_(Tchaikovsky,_Pyotr)) identifies this edition and editor (1854–1932).

The public distribution basis is the historical notation's **US public-domain status**: 1909 published content is beyond the maximum 95-year term; its renewal does not restart that term. The [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/) explains the published-work cutoff. This records a US basis, not worldwide legal certification or permission for a different edition.

We distribute our own editable transcription, arrangements and renders. **No source scan is bundled**, including the earlier 1948 Muzgiz scan. A prior 1948-based event listing was viewed only as a comparison aid during transcription; no old L2 or old MusicXML is loaded by the new builder. The identified Schirmer page governs readings.

The bounded agent audit covers pitches, rhythms, ties, five grace notes, four arpeggiation symbols and main phrasing/dynamics. Source fingering and pedal are excluded; some slur/direction attachment positions are interpretive. Human source review and critical-edition certification are NOT_RUN. Quarter = 52 is a comparison assumption.
